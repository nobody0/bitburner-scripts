> *"Posthuman"* · theme: `hacking`

**In-node:** **Intelligence** — a stat that is permanent and persistent, never
reset to 1. Also unlocks `ns.getBitNodeMultipliers()`, permanent Formulas.exe,
and BitNode multiplier info on the Stats page.

## Reading

- The mildest hacking node. `ServerMaxMoney` is **unchanged** — BN5 nerfs yield
  per hack, not the pool, so grow/weaken economics stay BN1-like.
- Infiltration is buffed on both money and rep: that is the node's hint about
  where to get rep while script income is down.

## SF5

Intelligence permanently, `getBitNodeMultipliers()`, permanent Formulas.exe, and
all hacking multipliers +8% / 12% / 14%.

## For our code

- Intelligence feeds `intBonus` in the hacking formulas.
  `sim/vendor/.../PersonObjects/formulas/intelligence.ts` is vendored — check
  `shared/formulas.ts` applies it, or sim and game diverge for any player
  carrying SF5.
- Infiltration is buffed here, but remains deliberately manual and outside the
  automation roster.

## Stock cross-node experiment

BN5 is the first non-BN8 stock benchmark. It is more informative than a first
BN12 run: early BN12 is essentially BN1 with small scaling penalties, while
BN5 cuts script hack income to 15% without changing stock-market costs or
returns. That makes stocks a plausible complement without making them the
node's mandatory mechanic.

The paired `bn5-hacking` and `bn5-hacking-stock` simulator profiles share the
same seed set and the same vendored omega-net midpoint. They start at $12b and
measure time to $20b. The treatment must buy WSE and TIX at their real $5.2b
combined cost; it receives no free market access. Only the stock feature flag
differs between the profiles.

Reference A/B result (real controller, three valid seeds, no unmodeled calls or
crashes):

| Seed | Hacking only | Hacking + stock | Improvement |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4.45h | 3.23h | 73.3m (27.4%) |
| 2 | 5.07h | 3.75h | 78.9m (25.9%) |
| 3 | 4.39h | 3.84h | 33.4m (12.7%) |
| median | 4.45h | 3.75h | 42.0m (15.7%) |

This proves the isolated mid-run capital phase, not full BN5 completion. It is
deliberately the minimum hacking + stock experiment; later BN5 progression
coverage can extend the terminal goal without changing the paired fixture.

This benchmark also constrains the implementation:

- unlock proposals report ROI against all capital required by the proposal,
  even when the next action spends only part of it;
- stock wealth snapshots combine cash and positions from one probe/action so
  an asynchronously stale player balance cannot complete the wealth goal;
- outside BN8, stock reserves its peak home RAM only after TIX is available,
  so an unavailable or rejected stock investment cannot tax hacking merely
  because the feature is enabled. BN8 retains the startup reserve because TIX
  is free there.
