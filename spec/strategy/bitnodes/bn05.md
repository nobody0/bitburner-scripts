# BN5 — Artificial Intelligence

> *"Posthuman"* · theme: `hacking`

**In-node:** **Intelligence** — a stat that is permanent and persistent, never
reset to 1. Also unlocks `ns.getBitNodeMultipliers()`, permanent Formulas.exe,
and BitNode multiplier info on the Stats page.

## Multipliers vs BN1

| Group | Changes |
|---|---|
| Servers | `ServerStartingSecurity` 2 · `ServerStartingMoney` 0.5 · `CloudServerSoftcap` 1.2 |
| Income | `ScriptHackMoney` **0.15** · `HacknetNodeMoney` 0.2 · `CrimeMoney` 0.5 · `DarknetMoneyMultiplier` 0.7 |
| Experience | `HackExpGain` 0.5 |
| Reset loop | `AugmentationMoneyCost` 2 |
| Buffs | `InfiltrationMoney` **1.5** · `InfiltrationRep` **1.5** · `StaneksGiftPowerMultiplier` 1.3 |
| Other features | `CorporationValuation` 0.75 · `CorporationDivisions` 0.75 · `GangUniqueAugs` 0.5 |
| Endgame | `WorldDaemonDifficulty` 1.5 → hacking 4500 |

## Reading

- The mildest hacking node. `ServerMaxMoney` is **unchanged** — BN5 nerfs yield
  per hack, not the pool, so grow/weaken economics stay BN1-like.
- Infiltration is buffed on both money and rep: that is the node's hint about
  where to get rep while script income is down.

## SF5

Intelligence permanently, `getBitNodeMultipliers()`, permanent Formulas.exe, and
all hacking multipliers +8% / 12% / 14%.

## For our code

- Intelligence feeds `intBonus` in the hacking formulas.
  `sim/vendor/.../PersonObjects/formulas/intelligence.ts` is vendored — check
  `shared/formulas.ts` applies it, or sim and game diverge for any player
  carrying SF5.
- `side`'s infiltration half is worth more here than anywhere except BN11, and
  it is the least automatable thing we own.
