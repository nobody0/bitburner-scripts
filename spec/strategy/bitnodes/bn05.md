# BN5 — Artificial Intelligence

> *"Posthuman"* · theme: [`hacking`](../features/hacking.md)

**In-node:** the **Intelligence** skill. It is unlocked by `bitNode === 5` or any
SF5 level, and it is the one skill a prestige does not reset: the other six are
set back to 1 while Intelligence is restored from the player's persistent
Intelligence experience (`sim/world.ts:354-382`, the save field
`persistentIntelligenceData.exp` — `shared/save/decode.ts:230,258-261`). It
enters the hacking formulas as `intBonus`; see
[`hacking.md`](../features/hacking.md).

BN5 or SF5 also unlocks `ns.getBitNodeMultipliers()`
(`types/NetscriptDefinitions.d.ts:8915`; 4 GB, which is why our own strategy
reads the pinned static table instead — `shared/features/bitnode.ts:100-106`),
and grants **Formulas.exe** on home at every prestige
(`sim/game-run.ts:835`; the re-grant rule is in
[`progression.md`](../features/progression.md)).

Sixteen fields differ from BN1. Verified against `shared/features/bitnode.ts:151-163`.

## SF5

Every level grants Intelligence, `getBitNodeMultipliers()` and Formulas.exe as
above. The multiplier part hits exactly six player mults — `hacking`,
`hacking_chance`, `hacking_speed`, `hacking_money`, `hacking_grow`,
`hacking_exp` — at a cumulative `+8% / 12% / 14%` for levels 1 / 2 / 3
(`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts:79-93`, `8/2^i` summed
over the levels held). These are totals, not per-level stacking bonuses.

## Notes

- BN5 leaves `ServerMaxMoney` at 1, so only per-hack yield is cut, not the pool.
  Only BN5, BN8, BN10 and BN12 do that.
- `InfiltrationRep` is changed in exactly two nodes: BN5 (1.5) and BN11 (2.5).
