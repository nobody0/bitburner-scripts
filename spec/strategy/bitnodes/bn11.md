# BN11 — The Big Crash

> *"Okay. Sell it all."* · theme: `career`

**In-node:** nothing new. A pure multiplier node about companies, crime and
infiltration in a collapsed economy.

## Reading

- Crime ×3 and infiltration ×2.5 on money *and* rep, against a 1% farm.
  `career` and `side` carry the run.
- `ServerWeakenRate: 2` is one of the few outright buffs in any multiplier table
  and is easy to miss: prep is cheap, so the farm **ramps** fast even though its
  ceiling is 1%.

## SF11

Company **favor raises both salary and reputation gain** by 1% per favor
(normally rep only). Plus:

| Level | Company salary/rep | Aug escalation reduction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +32% | −4% |
| 2 | +48% | −6% |
| 3 | +56% | −7% |

The escalation reduction applies to the ×1.9 `MultipleAugMultiplier`, which
makes SF11 quietly one of the best **reset-loop** Source-Files: it cheapens
large aug batches in every node.

## For our code

- `career` needs a real company-progression model: job selection, promotion
  requirements, favor accumulation across resets.
- `progression`'s batch-size optimizer must read SF11 level, since it changes
  the escalation constant.
- Decide whether infiltration is automatable at all. If not, BN11 should say so
  via `Feature.api` rather than showing an idle tab — this is the node where the
  gap costs the most.
