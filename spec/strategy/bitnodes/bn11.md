## SF11

At level 1 and above, company favor multiplies **salary as well as reputation**
by `1 + favor/100`; without SF11 that factor applies to reputation only. The
gate is level > 0, not per-level (`sim/features/companies.ts:130-137,154`).

Each level also adds `32 / 2^(level-1)` percent to both the `work_money` and
`company_rep` player multipliers (`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts:154-164`),
and discounts the augmentation queue escalation:

| Level | `work_money` · `company_rep` | Queue escalation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +32% | ×0.96 → 1.824 |
| 2 | +48% | ×0.94 → 1.786 |
| 3 | +56% | ×0.93 → 1.767 |

The escalation column multiplies `CONSTANTS.MultipleAugMultiplier` 1.9
(`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/Constants.ts:42`) by
`[1, 0.96, 0.94, 0.93][level]` (`shared/strategy/factions/augs.ts:37-38,151-153`);
the pricing formula is in [`progression`](../features/progression.md).

## Notes

- `ServerWeakenRate` 2 doubles the security removed per weaken thread
  (`shared/formulas.ts:182`), so prep costs half the weaken threads of BN1 even
  though `ServerMaxMoney` is 0.01. BN11 is the only node that sets this field;
  BN12 alone otherwise scales it, by SF12 level (`shared/features/bitnode.ts:315`).
