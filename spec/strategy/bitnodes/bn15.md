# BN15 — The Secrets of the Dark Net

> *"The rules have changed"* · theme: [`dnet`](../features/dnet.md)

**In-node:** the darknet. "Unlike the traditional network of servers, the 'dark'
net is a constantly shifting, complex, unreliable place where servers can move or
disappear at any moment. Long-distance communication is often impossible,
requiring scripts to be self-sufficient and durable, and spread themselves to
stay alive" — and access is bought with "the darknet servers' weak passwords and
leaky logs" (`src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx:531-535`). Destroying the node grants SF15,
or upgrades an existing SF15 to a maximum of level 3 (`:542-543`). The mechanic
itself is [`../../dnet.md`](../../dnet.md); the feature view, including the
access gates and the `DarknetMoneyMultiplier` spread across nodes, is
[`../features/dnet.md`](../features/dnet.md).

**The headline rule:** *"In this BitNode, the Daedalus faction has not yet found
and monopolized the fabled Red Pill augmentation"* (`:538`). BN15 is the only
node that moves the Pill forward in the labyrinth: when the next unclaimed reward
would be **The L4w of Bayes**, `getLabAugReward` returns The Red Pill instead
(`src/DarkNet/effects/labyrinth.ts:419-421`), so **four** labyrinth rewards
precede the Pill here rather than six, and it drops from the fifth lab,
`EternalLab` (`:449-455`).

**Mutation clock.** `getDarknetCyclesPerMutation` is
`(rateMultiplier × 150) / depth` cycles of 200 ms, and `rateMultiplier` is **1 in
BN15, 2 everywhere else** — the one node-specific term in the clock
(`src/DarkNet/utils/darknetNetworkUtils.ts:9-14`,
`shared/strategy/dnet/rates.ts:167-177`). At the default depth of 5 that is a
tick every ~6 s here against ~12 s elsewhere. Full clock:
[`../../dnet.md`](../../dnet.md).

## SF15

SF15 applies no multipliers of its own — every level is a code gate
(`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts:174-178`).

| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Permanently start with the TOR router and `DarkscapeNavigator.exe`, and unlock the full dark web in all BitNodes (`src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx:551-552`); `hasFullDarknetAccess` is `bitNodeN === 15 \|\| activeSourceFileLvl(15) > 0` (`src/DarkNet/effects/effects.ts:280`) |
| 2 | Charisma multiplies **company** salary and company rep by `getMultiplierFromCharisma(1.5)`, gated on `activeSourceFileLvl(15) > 1` (`src/Work/Formulas.ts:133,147-148`; those two `multWorkStats` args are the money and rep multipliers, `src/Work/WorkStats.ts:102-105`) |
| 3 | Charisma is added to the faction-work rep rate; `.cache` **money** ×1.5 (`src/DarkNet/effects/cacheFiles.ts:91-97`); and the authentication-time discount below |

**The auth-speed discount is level 3, not level 2.** The Source-File description
reads "Level 2: … Also increases authentication speed by 20%"
(`src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx:555`), and upstream even names the variable
`hasSf15_2Factor`, but the gate is `Player.activeSourceFileLvl(15) > 2 ? 0.8 : 1`
(`src/DarkNet/effects/effects.ts:80`, applied `:83`). Code wins; the description
is wrong upstream.

**The level-3 `.cache` bonus is money only.** The description says "xp and money
gained from .cache files by 50%", but the `1.5` factor exists in exactly one
place, `getMoneyReward` (`src/DarkNet/effects/cacheFiles.ts:91-97`), and no cache
reward path grants experience at all (`:35-73`).

SF15.3's reputation effect is transcribed at
`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/PersonObjects/formulas/Reputation.ts:66-71`: at level 3
and above charisma is added to the faction-work rate at **0.1×** for hacking work
(`:30`) and **0.3×** for security (`:45`) and field work (`:60`). In field work
that term sits inside the share-bonus factor and charisma also counts at 1×
separately; in security work it is outside it and unscaled.
