# BN10 — Digital Carbon

> *"Your body is not who you are"* · theme: [`sleeves`](../features/sleeves.md)

**In-node:**

- **Sleeves.** Buying a covenant sleeve and upgrading sleeve memory both require
  being in BN10 — **neither is possible in any other node**
  (`SleeveCovenantPurchases.tsx:30-47,87-98`, via
  [`sleeves`](../features/sleeves.md)). Entering BN10 also clamps every sleeve to
  shock ≤ 25 and sync ≥ 25 (`PlayerObjectGeneralMethods.ts:150-155`). The count
  formula, the one-per-target rules and the shock/sync rates live in
  [`sleeves`](../features/sleeves.md).
- **Grafting.** Installs an augmentation for money and time instead of faction
  reputation, at the cost of one entropy stack per graft. Gated by BN10 or SF10
  (`game/lib/probes/priced.ts:456`); starting one requires being in **New Tokyo**
  (`sim/features/grafting.ts:48`). Price, timing and the entropy penalty live in
  [`factions`](../features/factions.md).

## SF10

No multipliers: `applySourceFile.ts:149-152` is `case 10: // No effects, just
grants sleeves`. It unlocks the Sleeve API outside BN10
(`types/NetscriptDefinitions.d.ts:5720`) and the Grafting API
(`:6019`). Each level grants one sleeve, and the Source-File's contribution to
the count is capped at 3 — `min(3, sourceFileLvl(10) + (bitNodeN === 10 ? 1 : 0))`
(`SleeveCovenantPurchases.tsx:62-63`, quoted in
[`sleeves`](../features/sleeves.md)). So the BN10 `+1` changes nothing at
SF10.3 or above.

## Notes

- Every money source is 0.5 while `AugmentationMoneyCost` is 5: the reset loop
  costs 10× more per unit of income than in BN1.
- `AugmentationMoneyCost` 5 does **not** reach sleeve augmentations, which pay
  the raw `baseCost` ([`sleeves`](../features/sleeves.md)). `AugmentationRepCost`
  2 does reach them, via the sleeve-aug rep gate.
- `StaneksGiftExtraSize` −3 with `StaneksGiftPowerMultiplier` 0.75 — the gift is
  both smaller and weaker here.
