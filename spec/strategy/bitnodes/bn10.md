# BN10 — Digital Carbon

> *"Your body is not who you are"* · theme: `sleeves`

**In-node:**

- **Sleeves** — duplicate consciousnesses acting in parallel. You cannot buy
  sleeves *or upgrade them* outside this BitNode.
- **Grafting** — VitaLife, New Tokyo. Installs augmentations for money and time
  instead of faction rep, at the cost of accumulating **entropy**.

Sleeve count (`SleeveCovenantPurchases.tsx:62`):

```
min(3, SF10 level + (bitNodeN === 10 ? 1 : 0)) + sleevesFromCovenant
```

`MaxSleevesFromCovenant = 5`, and covenant sleeves cost `10^n × BaseCostPerSleeve`
— so the ceiling is **8**, and the last ones are priced to hurt.

## Reading

BN10 halves *everything* uniformly and multiplies aug money cost by 5. The
compensation is parallelism. The node asks: can N bodies out-earn the 5× tax?

**On "sleeves mean two jobs":** a sleeve's company work is gated on
`Player.jobs[companyName]` (`Sleeve.ts:410`) — the **player** must hold the job;
the sleeve then works it. `Player.jobs` is a record, so the capability is
**hold several jobs and have sleeves grind several company reputations at
once**.

But the parallelism is one-per-target, not N-per-target
(`NetscriptFunctions/Sleeve.ts:125, 152, 291`):

- **No two sleeves at the same company.**
- **No two sleeves at the same faction.**
- **No two sleeves on Bladeburner contracts.**
- A sleeve may not work your **gang's** faction at all.

The *player* is not part of these checks — only other sleeves — so player and
sleeve may share a company. Practically: sleeves widen the board by covering
*different* targets, they do not stack on one. A plan that puts three sleeves
on the highest-value faction throws.

## Grafting's entropy

Each graft adds 1 entropy, and entropy multiplies **every** player multiplier
by `EntropyEffect ^ entropy` with `EntropyEffect = 0.98` (`Constants.ts:104`,
applied in `Player.applyEntropy`). So it is a flat **−2% compounding on
everything per grafted augmentation** — not a per-stat or per-faction cost.

That makes grafting a genuinely global decision: the *n*-th graft costs
`1 - 0.98^n` of all prior progress, so grafting is strong early and
self-defeating in bulk. It also means `factions` cannot price a graft locally,
which is the tension [`spec/features.md`](../../features.md) flags in putting
grafting inside that feature.

## SF10

Unlocks Sleeve and Grafting APIs in other BitNodes. Each level grants one
sleeve (SF contribution caps at 3).

## For our code

- `sleeves` changes the **arbiter's shape**: the single `currentWork` slot
  becomes N+1 slots with different stats, shock and sync. The arbiter allocates
  one slot today.
- Shock/sync management is a real optimization problem and is unmodelled.
- Grafting lives in `factions` per `spec/features.md`; BN10 stress-tests that
  boundary, because entropy is a **global** multiplier penalty, not a
  faction-local cost.
