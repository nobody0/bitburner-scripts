# BN13 — They're lunatics

> *"1 step back, 2 steps forward"* · theme: [`stanek`](../features/stanek.md)

**In-node:** the **Church of the Machine God** in Chongqing
(`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/Locations/Enums.ts:21`) is joinable here, and grants
**Stanek's Gift** — mechanics in [`stanek`](../features/stanek.md).

Membership takes three `inviteReqs`
(`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/Faction/FactionTable.ts:1510-1531`): `bitNodeN` 13
**or** `sourceFile` 13 (`:1514-1521`), `numAugmentations` **0** (`:1524-1527`),
and being at the location (`:1528-1531`). `keepOnInstall` is `true` (`:1509`).
`numAugmentations: 0` counts installed **and** queued augmentations, excluding
NeuroFlux Governor (`shared/strategy/factions/requirements.ts:259-266`;
`types/NetscriptDefinitions.d.ts:6720-6722`). That requirement carries no BN13
exemption, so the sequencing rule — accept the Gift on a clean install, before
any other augmentation — holds in this node too. See `## Open` for the separate
question of an outright ban.

## SF13

Each level adds 1 to the Gift's base size:
`baseSize = 9 + StaneksGiftExtraSize + sourceFileLvl(13)`
(`sim/features/stanek.ts:50-54`, from v3.0.1 `StaneksGift.ts:22-31`; `BaseSize`
9 at `CotMG/data/Constants.ts:3`). `applySourceFile.ts:168-169` applies no
multiplier — "Grants more space on Stanek's Gift" is the whole effect. The
Source-File also satisfies the Church's first `inviteReq` outside BN13
(`FactionTable.ts:1518-1521`).

With **SF7 level 3**, joining Bladeburner immediately installs The Blade's
Simulacrum, so the Gift must be accepted first
([`bladeburner.md`](../features/bladeburner.md#sf73-forces-stanek-first),
[`stanek.md`](../features/stanek.md)).

## Notes

- `HackingLevelMultiplier` 0.25, `ServerStartingSecurity` 3 and both
  `FourSigmaMarketData*` at 10 are the game's extreme values for those fields.
- `HackExpGain` 0.1 is the second lowest, after BN9's 0.05.
- `CorporationValuation` 0.001 and `GangSoftcap` 0.3 are the lowest nonzero
  values of any node; BN8 alone sets both to 0.
- `StaneksGiftPowerMultiplier` 2 ties BN2 for the highest.
- BN13 changes 33 multiplier fields, more than any other node.
