# BN6 — Bladeburners

> *"Like Tears in Rain"* · theme: `bladeburner`

**In-node:** the **Bladeburner** division at the NSA — contracts, operations,
black ops, skills, city chaos, its own faction (rank 25) and its own
augmentations (18, tied for the largest single-faction pool).

**The ns API is available here.** `NetscriptFunctions/Bladeburner.ts` gates on
`canAccessBitNodeFeature(7) || canAccessBitNodeFeature(6)` — BN6 or SF6 is
sufficient. (This changed in 3.0.x; older notes claiming SF7 is required for the
API are wrong.)

## Reading

- `HackingLevelMultiplier` 0.35 × `HackExpGain` 0.25 is the harshest combined
  hacking nerf outside BN13. Hacking is not a path; combat stats and rank are.
- `DaedalusAugsRequirement` 35 closes the "skip everything, rush Daedalus"
  alternative.

## SF6

Access the Bladeburner division in other BitNodes. Raises both **level and exp
gain rate** of all combat stats by +8% / 12% / 14%.

## Bladeburner is a BitNode-destroy route — in every node, not just BN6

Completing **all 20 black operations** ends the BitNode on its own. Two
independent confirmations:

- `Bladeburner/ui/BlackOpPage.tsx:43` — with
  `numBlackOpsComplete >= numberOfBlackOperations`, a "Destroy w0r1d_d43m0n"
  button calls `finishBitNode()` and goes to the BitVerse.
- `NetscriptFunctions/Singularity.ts:1176` — `destroyW0r1dD43m0n` accepts
  `hackingRequirements() || bladeburnerRequirements()`, the latter being
  exactly that black-op count. **So the route is scriptable.**

It needs **no Red Pill, no Daedalus, and no hacking level whatsoever**. The
last black op, Operation Daedalus, requires **rank 400 000** and has base
difficulty 80 000 (`Bladeburner/data/BlackOperations.ts:704`).

That makes it a general alternative to the entire faction endgame anywhere we
hold SF6 or SF7 — not a BN6 curiosity. `shared/strategy/progression/endgame.ts`
models it as a first-class route for that reason.

## For our code

- Combat-stat acquisition (gym, crime, faction field work) becomes the primary
  `career` objective — a different objective shape from BN1's.
- The `bladeburner` gate (`inBladeburner()`, 0 GB) is right, but the driver
  needs real content: action selection, stamina and health management, and a
  rank plan that treats 400 000 as the actual target.
