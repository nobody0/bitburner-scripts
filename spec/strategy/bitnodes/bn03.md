# BN3 — Corporatocracy

> *"The Price of Civilization"* · theme: `corp`

**In-node:** create and manage a **corporation**. `canAccessCorporation()` is
`canAccessBitNodeFeature(3) && !bitNodeOptions.disableCorporation`. Being in BN3
**or** holding SF3 at exactly level 3 grants `WarehouseAPI` + `OfficeAPI` at
creation (`PlayerObjectCorporationMethods.ts:21`) — that is the "full API".

## Reading

- Every conventional income is ≤25% **and** augs cost 3× money and 3× rep. Only
  the corporation scales fast enough.
- The halved donation threshold is the node telling you how it expects rep to be
  bought: with corporate money, not with time.
- `HomeComputerRamCost` 1.5 and `CloudServerCost` 2 squeeze *our* architecture
  directly — dodge RAM and probe budget share the pool with the farm.

## SF3

Create corporations in other BitNodes (some disable it); **level 3 unlocks the
full API**. Charisma and company salary +8% / 12% / 14%.

## For our code

- `corp` is our least-developed feature and BN3 requires it to be excellent. It
  needs its own file, a strategy module and a simulator model.
- Below SF3.3 the corporation is only partially scriptable — the driver must
  degrade, not throw.
- `shared/ram/reserve.ts` must cope with 1.5× home RAM cost.
