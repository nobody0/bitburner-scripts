# BN3 — Corporatocracy

> *"The Price of Civilization"* · theme: [`corp`](../features/corp.md)

**In-node:** create and manage a **corporation**. `canAccessCorporation()` is
`canAccessBitNodeFeature(3) && !bitNodeOptions.disableCorporation`
(`src/PersonObjects/Player/PlayerObjectCorporationMethods.ts:8-10`). Two things
are BN3's alone:

- **The full API at creation.** BN3 **or** SF3 at exactly level 3 adds
  `WarehouseAPI` + `OfficeAPI` when the corporation is created. The test is an
  equality — `bitNodeN === 3 || activeSourceFileLvl(3) === 3` — so SF3.1 and
  SF3.2 grant nothing, and below that tier the two unlocks cost $50e9 each from
  corporation funds (`PlayerObjectCorporationMethods.ts:21-24`,
  `src/Corporation/data/CorporationUnlocks.ts:65-75`).
- **Seed money.** `bitNodeN !== 3 && !selfFund` returns
  `UseSeedMoneyOutsideBN3` (`src/Corporation/helpers.ts:37-39`), so seed funding
  — 500e6 investor shares of 1e9, 50% dilution, in place of $150e9 of the
  player's own cash — exists only here.

Everything else — cycle, valuation, dividends, investment rounds, the
degraded-API path — is in [`corp.md`](../features/corp.md).

**Citations.** `src/Corporation/`, `src/Work/` and `src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx`
are not in the vendor extract — `tools/vendor.ts:74` hash-pins
`Corporation/Corporation.ts` under `TRANSCRIPTION_SOURCE_PATHS` instead of
copying it. Bare `src/…` paths here are therefore the **checkout** at pinned
commit `3162fd2`, the fallback [`game-source.md`](../../game-source.md)
establishes; vendored files carry their full `sim/vendor/…` path.

## SF3

Lets you create corporations in other BitNodes, some of which disable the
mechanic; **level 3 permanently unlocks the full API**
(`src/BitNode/BitNode.tsx:128-134`).

The multiplier is `1 + mult/100` with `mult = Σ 8/2^i` over levels held:
**+8% / +12% / +14%**. It is applied to exactly two player multipliers,
`charisma` and `work_money`
(`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts:63-72`). `work_money`
scales company salary money and nothing else (`src/Work/Formulas.ts:147`), so
the description's "charisma and company salary multipliers" matches the code.

## Notes

- `CorporationValuation`, `CorporationSoftcap` and `CorporationDivisions` all
  stay at 1 here — the theme node does not nerf its own feature
  ([`corp.md`](../features/corp.md)).
- The 75-favor donation threshold is `floor(150 * FavorToDonateToFaction)`
  ([`factions.md`](../features/factions.md)).
- `HomeComputerRamCost` 1.5 and `CloudServerCost` 2 are read by the fleet
  planner as `costMultiplier` (`game/lib/features/hacking.ts:794-800`).
