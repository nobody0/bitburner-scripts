# BN12 — The Recursion

> *"Repeat."* · theme: [`progression`](../features/progression.md)

**In-node:** the only node whose multipliers are a *formula* rather than a
table — every field is a function of the SF12 level
(`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/BitNode/BitNodeMults.ts:362-433`, mirrored by
`bitNode12()` in `shared/features/bitnode.ts:305-333` and pinned field-by-field
by `sim/tests/bitnode-parity.test.ts:83-92`). The level that applies is the
**active** one: the owned SF12 level plus 1 while the current node is 12
(`sim/ns/api.ts:493-497`). Reading the owned level instead runs the node one
recursion level low (`shared/strategy/progression/endgame.ts:143-147`).

## Multipliers vs BN1

`lvl` is the active SF12 level:

```
inc = 1.02 ^ lvl        dec = 1 / inc
```

## SF12

Grants NeuroFlux Governor levels equal to the SF12 level and applies no player
multipliers of its own (`sim/vendor/bitburner/src/SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts:165-166`).
Our fixture encodes the SF12.30 benefit as one installed NeuroFlux object at
level 30 with its multiplier applied thirty times (`sim/profiles.ts:292-304`).

Those levels sit on **one** augmentation object — owned augmentations are a
name→level map (`types/NetscriptDefinitions.d.ts:93`) — so they add 1, not the
SF12 level, to the installed count Daedalus reads
(`shared/strategy/progression/eta.ts:293-298`; the upstream gate is cited in
[`progression`](../features/progression.md)). SF12 moves Daedalus's requirement
through the node multiplier instead.

## Notes

- Evaluated at `lvl = 0` every `inc`/`dec` is exactly 1, so BN12's fixed part is
  four nerfs (`ServerStartingSecurity` 1.5, `CorporationSoftcap` 0.8,
  `CorporationDivisions` 0.5, `GangSoftcap` 0.8) plus
  `DaedalusAugsRequirement` 31 and `StaneksGiftExtraSize` +1. A first BN12 run
  is not that: its active level is 1.
- `DaedalusAugsRequirement` is 31 for `lvl` 0–35 and only reaches its 40 cap at
  `lvl` ≥ 117, because `inc` must pass 10.
- Stanek improves with depth: `StaneksGiftPowerMultiplier` and the additive
  `StaneksGiftExtraSize` (BN1 default 0) both take `inc`, and both are
  `harderWhen: LOWER` (`shared/features/bitnode.ts:494-495`).
- Six fields stay at 1 at every level: `HackingSpeedMultiplier`,
  `ScriptHackMoneyGain`, `CrimeSuccessRate`, `CompanyWorkRepGain`, `GoPower`,
  `DarknetLabyrinthRewardsTheRedPill`. Batch wall-clock and whether hacked money
  reaches the player never change here; yields and skill levels do.

## Open

- Does SF12 have a maximum level? Nothing we hold states one: the vendored
  extract carries no source-file level cap, `bitNode12()` accepts any `lvl`, and
  the `min(30 + inc, 40)` cap only binds at `lvl` ≥ 117. Needs the `v3.0.1` tag.
