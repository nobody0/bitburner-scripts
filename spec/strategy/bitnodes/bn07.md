# BN7 — Bladeburners 2079

> *"More human than humans"* · theme: `bladeburner`

**In-node:** the same Bladeburner division as BN6 — in 3.0.1 the two node
descriptions are identical, and the ns API is available in either
(`canAccessBitNodeFeature(7) || canAccessBitNodeFeature(6)`). BN7 is not the
"API node"; it is the **harder** Bladeburner node, and the SF is worth more.

## Reading

BN7 is BN6 with the **theme feature itself** made expensive: rank 0.6×, skill
costs 2×, augs 3× money. That is the pattern the harder nodes use — give you the
tool, then make the tool costly.

## SF7

All Bladeburner multipliers +8% / 12% / 14%. **Level 3 additionally grants The
Blade's Simulacrum augmentation immediately on joining the division.**

That last clause has a cross-feature consequence: it installs an augmentation
for you, and owning any non-NeuroFlux aug bans the Church of the Machine God
outside BN13. BN13's own SF text spells it out — **with SF7.3 you must accept
Stanek's Gift before joining Bladeburner.** See [`bn13.md`](bn13.md).

## For our code

- Skill-point allocation must read `BladeburnerSkillCost`; a plan tuned at 1×
  misallocates badly at 2×.
- The SF7.3 → Stanek ordering constraint is a **cross-feature sequencing rule**,
  not a capability gate. Nothing in our model expresses that today.
- Chaos and population estimation, and city selection, are unmodelled.
