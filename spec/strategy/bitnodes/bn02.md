# BN2 — Rise of the Underworld

> *"From the shadows, they rose"* · theme: `gang`

**In-node:** seven factions can found a **gang** — Slum Snakes, Tetrads, The
Syndicate, The Dark Army, Speakers for the Dead, **NiteSec**, **The Black Hand**
(`GangConstants.Names`). `canAccessGang()` returns `true` unconditionally when
`bitNodeN === 2`; elsewhere it needs SF2 **and** karma ≤ −54 000. The gang
faction offers more augmentations than any other — and **in BN2 it offers The
Red Pill**, which is the node's intended finish.

## Multipliers vs BN1

| Group | Changes |
|---|---|
| Hacking | `HackingLevelMultiplier` 0.8 |
| Servers | `ServerMaxMoney` **0.08** · `ServerStartingMoney` 0.4 · `ServerGrowthRate` 0.8 · `CloudServerSoftcap` 1.3 |
| Income | `CrimeMoney` **3** · `InfiltrationMoney` **3** |
| Reputation | `FactionPassiveRepGain` **0** · `FactionWorkRepGain` 0.5 |
| Other features | `CorporationSoftcap` 0.9 · `CorporationDivisions` 0.9 · `StaneksGiftPowerMultiplier` 2 · `StaneksGiftExtraSize` −6 |
| Endgame | `WorldDaemonDifficulty` **5** → hacking 15 000 |

## Reading

- The farm is worth ~1/12th and crime is tripled: **stop farming for money,
  commit crime.**
- Passive rep is zero and worked rep halved: **get rep from the gang, not from
  faction work.**
- Hacking 15 000 at 0.8× level gain puts the direct finish out of reach. The
  gang's Red Pill is the route.
- **The fastest gang is a hacking play.** Five of the seven gang factions have
  karma gates (−9 … −90), but NiteSec and The Black Hand are *backdoor*
  factions. Backdoor `avmnite-02h` or `I.I.I.I` and found a gang at zero karma.

## SF2

Form gangs in other BitNodes once karma ≤ −54 000. Crime success rate, crime
money and charisma +24% / 36% / 42%.

## For our code

- `gang` must run as **primary income and rep engine**, not a side channel — it
  currently shares `features/remaining.ts`.
- `career`'s crime selection must respond to `CrimeMoney: 3` and to karma
  buying nothing in-node.
- The farm planner must not thrash on 0.08× servers.

### What the backdoor route costs

Augmentation pool by gang faction (`AugmentationTable.ts`):

| Faction | Augs | Karma gate |
|---|---|---|
| The Syndicate | **18** | −90 (+ combat 200, $10m, Aevum/Sector-12) |
| The Dark Army | 15 | −45 (+ combat 300, hacking 300, 5 kills, Chongqing) |
| Speakers for the Dead | 13 | −45 (+ combat 300, hacking 100, 30 kills) |
| **NiteSec** | 11 | **none** — backdoor `avmnite-02h` |
| **The Black Hand** | 11 | **none** — backdoor `I.I.I.I` |
| Slum Snakes | 8 | −9 |
| Tetrads | 7 | −18 |

The trade is explicit: the karma-free backdoor route gives up **7
augmentations** against The Syndicate. Whether that is worth the karma and
combat grind under `CrimeMoney: 3` is now a well-posed question, which is what
makes it a good early simulator target.
