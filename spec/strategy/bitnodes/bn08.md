# BN8 — Ghost of Wall Street

> *"Money never sleeps"* · theme: [`stock`](../features/stock.md)

**In-node:** money starts at **$250 000 000** flat, replacing the augmentation-derived
starting cash (`sim/world.ts:394`). A WSE account and TIX API access are granted free
for the whole node, and re-granted at every prestige to an owner of SF8 at any level
(`sim/game-run.ts:487-493`). Shorts (`ns.stock.buyShort` / `sellShort`) and limit/stop
orders (`ns.stock.placeOrder` / `getOrders`) both work in-node with no Source-File
(`game/lib/features/stock.ts:263`, `game/lib/probes/dodged.ts:968`).

## SF8

| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Permanent WSE account + TIX API access at every prestige |
| 2 | Short stocks in other BitNodes |
| 3 | Limit/stop orders in other BitNodes |

The only multiplier is `Player.mults.hacking_grow`, ×1.12 / 1.18 / 1.21 at levels
1 / 2 / 3 — the `12 / 2^i` series in `SourceFile/applySourceFile.ts:124-133`. Nothing
in SF8 touches price, forecast or the market.

## Notes

- `ScriptHackMoney` 0.3 **and** `ScriptHackMoneyGain` 0 together: the server loses the
  money and the player receives none. Hacking survives only as experience and as price
  movement, since the influence roll measures `moneyDrained` before the player's cut.
  See [`../features/hacking.md`](../features/hacking.md) and
  [`../features/stock.md`](../features/stock.md).
