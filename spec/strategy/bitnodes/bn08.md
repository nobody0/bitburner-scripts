## Reading

- `ScriptHackMoney` 0.3 **and** `ScriptHackMoneyGain` 0 together: the server
  loses money and you gain none. Hacking survives only as **experience** and as
  **stock manipulation** — hacking drives a symbol down, growing drives it up.
  That is the node's actual mechanic.
- The finish line is BN1's, but every route to it except the market is closed.
- `FavorToDonateToFaction` 0 means all rep is buyable from turn one, so the
  reset loop collapses to "trade, donate, buy augs".

## For our code

Implemented. What the node demands and where it lives:

- **A real trading strategy.** `shared/strategy/stock/` — `market.ts` models the
  price tick (the shared volatility roll, the 75-tick regime cycle, the spread,
  the second-order forecast), `history.ts` recovers a forecast and the cycle clock
  from prices alone, `decide.ts` spends money. The node's free WSE + TIX means the
  ladder starts at the $25b 4S API, which is the one 4S purchase a script can use.
- **Shorts.** Available in-node without a source file, so `canShort` is true and
  the solver takes the bearish half of the book.
- **Hack/grow-as-manipulation coordination.** `stock` publishes a per-host intent
  and a dollar value per influencing op; `solveCycle` adds it to the same
  `$/GB/sec` target score, and the dispatcher sets `{stock: true}` on the grow for
  a long and the hack for a short — never both, since their successful
  steady-state influences oppose each other. The arbiter
  does not need to model the contention because it is priced INTO the score both
  sides already compare on. See [spec/targeting.md](../../targeting.md).
- **`ScriptHackMoneyGain: 0` is now a first-class input**, not an omission.
  `HackContext.scriptHackMoneyGain` scales the hacking income term and NOT the
  manipulation term, because influence is computed from `moneyDrained` before the
  player's cut. Without it every BN8 target reported as profitable while earning
  nothing. The farm keeps running here for two real reasons — experience, and
  price movement.
- **`stock:sole-income` priority.** When `manipulationLeverage` is infinite (the
  cut is zero), a position claim outranks hacknet upgrades and home-RAM
  investments instead of sitting at the bottom of the table. The augmentation fund
  still wins: in BN8 as everywhere else, the money exists to become permanent
  multipliers.
- **`FavorToDonateToFaction: 0`** means all rep is buyable from turn one, so the
  liquidation rule matters more here than anywhere: cash out, donate, install.
